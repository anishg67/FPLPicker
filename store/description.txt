Fantasy Premier League gives you 15 places, £100m and roughly 700 players to choose from. FPL Picker does the hard part.

A FULL SQUAD IN SECONDS
Tap once for a complete, legal squad: 2 goalkeepers, 5 defenders, 5 midfielders, 3 forwards, never more than 3 from one club, and the budget put to work. Your starting XI, formation, captain, vice-captain and bench order come with it.

BUILT ON LIVE DATA
Every player is rated on current prices, recent form, expected goals and assists, clean sheet and save rates, minutes played, injury news, and the difficulty of the next few fixtures.

ALREADY HAVE A TEAM?
Import your squad with your FPL team ID, or enter your 15 by hand. Tell the app your bank and free transfers, and it shows which moves are worth making — including whether a −4 hit pays for itself.

AS MUCH CONTROL AS YOU WANT
A short survey asks how much football you watch and how much you want to decide yourself. New managers get a finished team and plain English. Experienced ones get risk appetite, fixture horizon, form weighting, per-club limits, must-have players and a blocklist.

CHANGE ANYTHING BY HAND
Swap a player, bench someone, move the armband. Every change is checked against the game's rules, so you can't end up with an illegal squad — and the app tells you why a player is unavailable rather than hiding them.

NO JARGON REQUIRED
A built-in glossary explains xG, xA, FDR, effective ownership, hits, price changes and the rest in plain English, whenever you want it.

ALSO INCLUDED
• Save as many teams as you like and reload them at today's prices
• Share your squad as an image or as text
• Five colour themes and adjustable card detail
• No account, no ads, no tracking — nothing leaves your device

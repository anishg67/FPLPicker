REGULATED INDUSTRY

Squad Picker does not operate in a regulated industry. It is a planning and
analysis tool for Fantasy Premier League, a free-to-play game run by the
Premier League. The app takes no entries, holds no funds, processes no
payments, awards no prizes and offers no wagering or real-money gaming of any
kind. There is no gambling, lottery or contest functionality, so the licensing
requirements that apply to real-money fantasy sports do not apply here.

The app also collects no personal data, requires no account, and has no
in-app purchases, so no financial, health or children's-data regulation is
engaged.

THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL

The app displays publicly available Fantasy Premier League game data: player
names, in-game prices, match statistics, injury status and fixture difficulty
ratings. This is retrieved at runtime from the Premier League's own public API
(fantasy.premierleague.com/api), which requires no key, account, agreement or
authentication to access. Data is read only; nothing is written back.

The developer holds no licence from, and claims no affiliation with, the
Premier League or Fantasy Premier League. The app states this explicitly in
three places: its App Store description, its in-app Settings screen, and its
privacy policy.

The app does NOT use:
- club crests, badges or logos (clubs are shown as plain coloured circles with
  their three-letter abbreviations)
- Premier League or Fantasy Premier League logos, wordmarks or branding
- match footage, broadcast content or audio
- any user-generated content
